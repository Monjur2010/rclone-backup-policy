# Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026.

## Summary

This is a personal tool used by one person, Monjur Hassan, on his own devices, with his own Google account. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share anyone's data.

## What the app accesses

The app uses Google Drive API permissions to read files on my own computer and write copies of them into my own Google Drive. It only ever accesses the Google account of the person running it, which is me.

## What data is collected

None. There is no server, no database, no analytics, and no telemetry. Nothing is sent anywhere except to my own Google Drive.

## What data is shared

Nothing is shared with any third party. The only other party involved is Google, which stores the files in my own Drive under Google's own terms.

## Data retention

Copies stay in my Google Drive until I delete them. I can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google account permissions page.

## Children

This tool is not directed at children and has no users other than me.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
